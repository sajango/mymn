# WAVE 4 ENTRY SIGNALS

## Entry Overview

Wave 4 completion signals the beginning of Wave 5 - the **final leg** of the impulse. Entry here captures the trend continuation before completion.

## Wave 4 Characteristics

| Attribute | Typical Value | Validation |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Retracement | 23.6% - 38.2% of Wave 3 | Shallower than Wave 2 |
| Overlap Rule | Cannot enter Wave 1 territory | Exception: Ending diagonal |
| Pattern Type | Often flat, triangle | Alternates with Wave 2 |
| Duration | Usually longer than Wave 2 | Time correction |

## Entry Criteria Checklist

**All conditions MUST be met:**

1. **Fibonacci Retracement**
   - Price reaches 38.2% - 61.8% of Wave 3
   - Most common: 38.2% (shallow) to 50%
   - Deep corrections (61.8%) less frequent

2. **RSI Confirmation**
   - RSI in 45-55 range
   - Stays above 40 in strong trend
   - No return to oversold territory

3. **Wave 1 Territory Rule**
   - Wave 4 low MUST stay above Wave 1 high
   - Violation invalidates standard impulse count
   - Exception only for ending diagonal patterns

4. **EMA Support**
   - Price pulls back to EMA 34
   - Rarely touches EMA 89 (only deep corrections)
   - EMA acts as dynamic support

5. **MACD Histogram**
   - Histogram shrinking toward zero
   - Should stay **positive** in uptrend
   - Signals correction nearing end

## Stop-Loss Placement

| Method | Rule | ATR Alternative |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| Primary | Below Wave 4 low | Entry - (ATR × 2.0) |
| Structure | Below Wave 1 high | Entry - (ATR × 1.5) |

**Critical**: Wave 4 entering Wave 1 territory invalidates count (unless ending diagonal).

## Take-Profit Targets (Wave 5)

Wave 5 targets depend on **whether Wave 3 was extended**:

### Normal Wave 3 Targets

| Target | Fib Level | Probability | Close % |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| TP1 | 61.8% of W1 | 80% | 40% |
| TP2 | 100% of W1 | 65% | 35% |
| TP3 | 61.8% of W3 | 50% | 25% |

### If Wave 3 Extended (>200% of W1)

| Target | Fib Level | Probability | Close % |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| TP1 | 61.8% of W1 | 75% | 50% |
| TP2 | 100% of W1 | 55% | 35% |
| TP3 | Wave 3 high | 40% | 15% |

**Warning**: Extended Wave 3 often leads to truncated Wave 5.

## Risk:Reward Expectations

| Scenario | Typical R:R | Notes |

| Conservative (TP1) | 1:1.5 | High probability |
| Standard (TP2) | 1:2.0 | Good balance |
| Extended (TP3) | 1:2.5 | Watch for truncation |

## False Signal Filters

**DO NOT enter if:**

1. Wave 4 enters Wave 1 territory (unless diagonal confirmed)
2. RSI drops below 40 - weak trend
3. MACD crosses below zero - momentum lost
4. Wave 3 was not clearly strongest wave
5. Price breaks significantly below EMA 89

## Pattern Types in Wave 4

| Pattern | Probability | Structure |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Flat | 35% | A-B-C sideways |
| Triangle | 30% | A-B-C-D-E contracting |
| Zigzag | 25% | A-B-C sharp |

**Alternation**: If Wave 2 was sharp (zigzag), expect Wave 4 to be flat/triangle.

## Truncation Risk Assessment

After **extended Wave 3** (>200% of W1):
- Wave 5 often truncates (fails to exceed Wave 3)
- Use conservative targets (TP1-TP2 only)
- Tighten stops after TP1 hit
- Consider smaller position size

## Wave 4 Triangle Recognition

Triangles are common in Wave 4 position:

### Structure
```
        B
       /│\
      / │ \D
     /  │  │\
    A   │  │ \
        │  │  E
        C  │
           └─► Breakout to Wave 5
```

### Triangle Characteristics
- 5 internal waves (A-B-C-D-E)
- Each wave smaller than previous
- Converging trendlines
- Wave E often undershoots

### Trading Triangle in Wave 4
1. **Identify**: Look for overlapping A-B-C-D structure
2. **Wait**: For E wave to complete
3. **Entry**: On breakout in trend direction
4. **Target**: Measure widest part, project from breakout

## MACD Behavior in Wave 4

```
Wave 3 Peak: MACD histogram at maximum
Wave 4:     Histogram shrinks but stays POSITIVE
            Zero line acts as support
            Cross below zero = Warning (momentum lost)
Entry:      When histogram stops shrinking, starts rising
```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Ignoring overlap**: Wave 4 entering Wave 1 territory invalidates count
2. **Expecting deep correction**: Wave 4 is typically shallower than Wave 2
3. **Missing triangle**: Sideways action may be triangle forming
4. **Ignoring extended W3**: After 200%+ Wave 3, expect truncated Wave 5
5. **Wrong ATR multiplier**: Wave 4 stops are tighter (2.0x ATR vs 2.5x)

## Ending Diagonal Exception

If Wave 4 **does** enter Wave 1 territory:
1. Check if structure is ending diagonal
2. Diagonal characteristics:
   - All sub-waves are 3-wave structures
   - Overlapping waves (4 enters 1)
   - Converging trendlines
3. If confirmed: Trade continues with adjusted targets
4. Expect **deep correction** after diagonal completes

## Wave 5 Truncation Warning Signs

After Wave 4, assess truncation risk:

| Condition | Truncation Risk | Action |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| W3 < 200% of W1 | Low | Full targets |
| W3 = 200-250% of W1 | Medium | Reduce TP3 |
| W3 > 250% of W1 | High | TP1-TP2 only |
| RSI divergence at W3 | High | Conservative exit |
