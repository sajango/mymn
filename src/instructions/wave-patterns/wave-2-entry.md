# WAVE 2 ENTRY SIGNALS

## Entry Overview

Wave 2 completion signals the beginning of Wave 3 - typically the **strongest and longest** wave in an impulse sequence. This is a high-probability entry opportunity.

## Wave 2 Characteristics

| Attribute | Typical Value | Validation |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Retracement | 50% - 78.6% of Wave 1 | Most common: 61.8% |
| Maximum | Cannot exceed Wave 1 start | 100% = INVALIDATION |
| Volume | Lower than Wave 1 | Decreasing during correction |
| RSI | 40 - 50 range | Should NOT return to oversold |

## Entry Criteria Checklist

**All conditions MUST be met:**

1. **Fibonacci Retracement**
   - Price reaches 50% - 78.6% of Wave 1
   - Ideal zone: 61.8% - 78.6% (highest probability)

2. **RSI Confirmation**
   - RSI in 35-50 range (not oversold)
   - RSI should NOT return below 30
   - Room to rise indicates momentum potential

3. **Volume Profile**
   - Volume decreasing during correction
   - Low volume at Wave 2 low = exhaustion

4. **EMA Support**
   - Price near EMA 34 or EMA 89
   - These act as dynamic support zones
   - Pullback to EMA = key entry zone

5. **MACD Histogram**
   - Histogram shrinking (correction ending)
   - Stays above zero in strong uptrend
   - Signal line approaching histogram

## Stop-Loss Placement

| Method | Rule | ATR Alternative |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| Primary | Below Wave 2 low | Entry - (ATR × 2.5) |
| Tight | Below 78.6% level | Entry - (ATR × 2.0) |

**Critical**: If Wave 2 low is breached, the count is INVALID.

## Take-Profit Targets (Wave 3)

Wave 3 typically extends **161.8% - 261.8%** of Wave 1 length.

| Target | Fib Level | Probability | Close % |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| TP1 | 100% of W1 | 85% | 40% |
| TP2 | 161.8% of W1 | 70% | 35% |
| TP3 | 261.8% of W1 | 45% | 25% |

**Note**: TP1 must exceed Wave 1 end - Wave 3 is never the shortest.

## False Signal Filters

**DO NOT enter if:**

1. RSI returns to oversold (<30) - may not be Wave 2
2. Wave 2 retraces >100% - count invalidated
3. Volume increasing during correction - may be impulsive move
4. Price breaks below EMA 89 significantly
5. MACD crosses below zero (strong downward momentum)

## Pattern Alternation

If Wave 2 is a **sharp zigzag**:
- Expect Wave 4 to be **flat or triangle**
- This alternation principle has ~80% probability

## Wave 2 Pattern Types

### Sharp Correction (Zigzag)
- Structure: A-B-C where A and C are 5-wave moves
- Typically retraces 61.8% - 78.6%
- Fast, steep decline
- Followed by sideways Wave 4 (alternation)

### Flat Correction
- Structure: A-B-C where all are 3-wave moves
- B wave returns to or exceeds Wave 1 end
- Shallower retracement (38.2% - 50%)
- Followed by sharp Wave 4 (alternation)

### Complex (Double/Triple)
- Structure: W-X-Y or W-X-Y-X-Z
- Time-consuming, confusing
- Lower confidence for entry
- Wait for clear completion

```
Wave 1: 3200 → 3280 (+80 points)
Wave 2: 3280 → 3231 (retraced 61.2% to Fib 61.8)
RSI at Wave 2 low: 42 (optimal zone)
EMA 34: 3235 (price near support)
Volume: Declining through Wave 2
Signal: BUY at 3232, SL: 3195, TP1: 3312, TP2: 3361
```

### Failed Signal Example
```
Wave 1: 3200 → 3280 (+80 points)
Wave 2: 3280 → 3198 (retraced 102.5% - INVALIDATED)
Result: Count invalid - Wave 2 broke Wave 1 origin
Action: Re-analyze wave structure
```

### Low Confidence Scenario
```
Wave 1: 3200 → 3280 (+80 points)
Wave 2: 3280 → 3256 (retraced only 30%)
RSI at Wave 2 low: 55 (too high)
Result: Shallow correction - may not be Wave 2
Action: WAIT for deeper retracement or re-count
```

## MACD Pattern During Wave 2

```
Wave 1 Peak: MACD histogram peaks (positive)
Wave 2:     MACD histogram declines toward zero
            Should NOT cross significantly negative
Entry:      When histogram starts to curl up from lows
```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Entering too early**: Wait for retracement to reach 50%+ zone
2. **Ignoring RSI**: Oversold RSI at Wave 2 suggests deeper correction coming
3. **Missing volume**: Increasing volume during pullback is NOT corrective
4. **Wrong timeframe**: Ensure wave count on primary TF, not just entry TF
5. **Forcing count**: If structure unclear, assign "Ambiguous" and reduce confidence
